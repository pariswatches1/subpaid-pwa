# SubPaid Product Strategy: Best-in-Class Differentiation

## Executive Summary

Based on comprehensive competitive analysis of Buildertrend, Procore, Jobber, ServiceTitan, and 6 other major players, SubPaid has a massive opportunity to dominate the **subcontractor segment** - the fastest-growing market at 11.6% CAGR. While competitors build for General Contractors first, SubPaid should be **purpose-built for trade contractors** who face the industry's worst pain points.

**Key Opportunity:** Construction has the slowest payment cycle of any U.S. industry (74-94 days average), costing $280 billion annually. No competitor fully solves this.

**For trade contractors and subcontractors** who struggle with slow payments, complex compliance, and unreliable GCs,

**SubPaid** is the only financial platform **purpose-built for subs** that combines instant payment, automated lien protection, and GC reputation intelligence.

**Unlike** Buildertrend, Procore, and Jobber which are designed for general contractors or service businesses,

**SubPaid** understands that **subs face the hardest cash flow challenges in construction** and provides the tools to get paid faster, protect payment rights automatically, and bid more profitably.
